## 3. Executive Summary Text for Submission Portal

*(Word count: ~250 words — Ideal for copying into the "Methodology / Approach" field on Unstop)*

> **PRESSUREIQ** is a context-aware cricket performance and reliability intelligence framework developed for the SupeRR Selector Season 2 competition using ball-by-ball delivery records from the IPL and Men's T20Is (2022–2026, 200,999 deliveries across 867 matches).
>
> Rather than relying on unadjusted aggregates (raw strike rates or career economy, which suffer from low-stakes stat-padding), PRESSUREIQ defines pressure as contextual match difficulty on a continuous 0–100 scale: **Batting Pressure Index (BPI)** and **Bowling Pressure Index (BoPI)**. Contextual expectations ($E[\text{runs}]$, $P(\text{dismissal})$, $P(\text{boundary})$) are generated using strictly chronological walk-forward out-of-fold LightGBM models with probability calibration ($Brier \approx 0.05, ECE < 0.009$) and zero future or same-match leakage. Shortened matches incorporate dynamic legal-ball allocations for both innings.
>
> Delivery-level **Pressure-Adjusted Value Added (PAVA)** measures pure unamplified execution surplus (runs/ball) to avoid double-counting pressure, with complete accounting of illegal deliveries (charging wides/no-balls to bowlers). Player reliability is evaluated across **Five Quantitative Pillars**: Absolute Pressure PAVA, Baseline Retention Delta, Opportunity-Adjusted Consistency, Context Adaptability (IPL vs T20I), and Empirical Bayes Shrinkage ($K=45$ pseudo-balls calibrated from variance ratios). Furthermore, **Pressure Response Curves (PRC)** classify players into empirical archetypes with statistical significance gating.
>
> Ranking stability is verified through **2,000 match-clustered bootstrap resamples** with exact scoring mathematical equivalence ($k=\mathbf{1}$ reproduces baseline scores to $<0.05$). The complete framework includes an interactive Streamlit/Plotly dashboard, exhaustive audit reports, and a 44-test verification suite passing with 100% success.

## 4. Key Talking Points for Presentation / Interview Round

1. **Why Raw Strike Rate and Economy are Misleading**:
   - In our ablation study, raw career strike rate has only a **30% overlap** (Jaccard similarity 0.176) with pressure reliability rankings. Traditional metrics reward Powerplay harvesting against field restrictions and low-pressure dead rubbers, whereas pressure reliability measures rate value added when wickets have fallen or required run rates exceed 10.0 RPO.
2. **Empirical Bayes Shrinkage ($K=45$)**:
   - Small-sample anomalies (e.g., a batter who hit 2 sixes in 5 pressure balls) are rigorously regressed toward the prior mean, preventing flash-in-the-pan players from displacing established match-winners like Suryakumar Yadav or Jos Buttler.
3. **Decoupled Pressure Indices (BPI vs BoPI)**:
   - Batting and bowling pressure are fundamentally asymmetric: a chasing batting side faces extreme pressure at 12 RRR with 2 wickets in hand, while the bowling side faces high urgency defending small margins against aggressive middle orders. Our decoupled indices reflect true tactical reality.
4. **Resolution of PAVA Pressure Amplification**:
   - By eliminating double-counting (since ML models already incorporate BPI/BoPI as features), PAVA cleanly isolates pure execution surplus ($V_{\text{raw}}$ in runs/ball), providing unbiased retention deltas and statistically sound ranking scores.
5. **Statistical Uncertainty Disclosure**:
   - Integer ranks represent expected values from 2,000 match-clustered resamples. We openly present 95% confidence intervals and top-10 inclusion probabilities, demonstrating scientific integrity rather than false deterministic precision.
