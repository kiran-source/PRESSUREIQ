## 2. Venue Telemetry: Sawai Mansingh Stadium (Jaipur)

Rajasthan Royals play 7 home league matches at Sawai Mansingh Stadium (SMS), Jaipur. Designing a recruitment strategy without ground-specific calibration produces sub-optimal squads:

- **Boundary Dimensions:**  
  * Square Boundaries: **75m to 78m** (among the largest in the IPL).  
  * Straight Boundaries: **70m to 72m**.  
- **Surface Behavior:** Red-soil and clay base that grips significantly in the middle overs (overs 7–15) before flattening out under dew.
- **Tactical Implication 1 ("Jaipur Squeeze"):** Large square boundaries heavily penalize wild sloggers and reward defensive finger-spinners who bowl flat, skiddy trajectories into the pitch, forcing batsmen to hit along the ground or risk boundary catches.
- **Tactical Implication 2 (Pace Execution):** High pace without movement travels straight; fast bowlers must possess deceptive changes of pace, back-of-the-hand slower deliveries, and wide-yorker execution.
- **Tactical Implication 3 (Away Fixtures):** Small venues (Wankhede, Chinnaswamy, Eden Gardens) require a dedicated No. 7 power finisher who can clear 80m boundaries against 140kph+ bowling from ball one.

## 4. Quota and Roster Balance Analysis

- **Overseas Slots:** IPL regulations permit a maximum of 8 overseas players in the squad (and 4 in the playing XI).
  * Current Rostered Overseas: **3 Players** (Jofra Archer, Shimron Hetmyer, Sam Curran).
  * Open Overseas Slots Available in Auction: **5 Slots**.
  * Strategic Allocation: RR should deploy 2 overseas slots on specialist starters (e.g., Liam Dawson and Spencer Johnson) and 3 overseas slots on high-upside backups (Jamie Smith, Jacob Bethell, Fazalhaq Farooqi).
- **Domestic Slots:** With 8 domestic players confirmed, RR can target 8–10 Indian domestic specialists in the auction to complete a 25-man squad, prioritizing domestic death bowling (Rasikh Salam, Prince Yadav) and lower-order power (Ashutosh Sharma, Nehal Wadhera).
