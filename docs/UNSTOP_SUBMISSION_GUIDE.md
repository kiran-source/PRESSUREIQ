## 3. Executive Summary Text for Submission Portal

> **PRESSUREIQ** is a context-aware cricket performance and reliability intelligence framework developed for the SupeRR Selector Season 2 competition using ball-by-ball delivery records from the IPL and Men's T20Is (2022–2026, 200,999 deliveries across 867 matches).
>
> Rather than relying on unadjusted aggregates (raw strike rates or career averages, which suffer from low-stakes stat-padding), PRESSUREIQ defines pressure as contextual environmental difficulty on a continuous 0–100 scale: **Batting Pressure Index (BPI)** and **Bowling Pressure Index (BoPI)**. Contextual baselines ($E[\text{runs}]$, $P(\text{dismissal})$, $P(\text{boundary})$) are established using chronological machine learning models (LightGBM) maintaining strict probability calibration ($Brier \approx 0.05$).
>
> Delivery-level **Pressure-Adjusted Value Added (PAVA)** is aggregated across independent matches and decomposed into **Five Quantitative Pillars**: Absolute Pressure Value, Baseline Retention Delta ($\Delta_{\text{high}} - \Delta_{\text{low}}$), Match-to-Match Consistency, Context Adaptability (IPL vs T20I), and Empirical Bayes Shrinkage ($K=45$ pseudo-balls) to eliminate small-sample noise. Furthermore, continuous **Pressure Response Curves (PRC)** classify players into behavioural archetypes, confirming all top-ranked players as *Clutch Climbers*.
>
> All models enforce **strict temporal causality** (zero future or post-match leakage) and full compliance with MCC Laws. Ranking stability is validated through match-clustered bootstrap resampling ($N=30$) to derive 95% confidence intervals. The complete framework includes an interactive Streamlit/Plotly dashboard, comprehensive dossiers, and a 14-test verification suite passing with 100% success.

## 4. Key Talking Points for Presentation / Interview Round

1. **Why Raw Strike Rate is Misleading**:
   - In our ablation study, raw career strike rate has only a **10% overlap** with pressure reliability rankings. Traditional strike rate rewards Powerplay harvesting against field restrictions, whereas pressure execution requires hitting defensive yorkers when wickets have collapsed.
2. **Empirical Bayes Shrinkage**:
   - Players with small sample sizes (e.g. 10 balls of lucky sixes) are regressed to the empirical population mean ($K=45$), ensuring that only players with repeated, statistically verifiable clutch execution rank in the top 10.
3. **Pace vs Spin Under Pressure**:
   - While express pace (Nathan Ellis, Matheesha Pathirana, Haris Rauf) dominates the death overs, defensive left-arm spin (Liam Dawson, Sufiyan Muqeem, Harpreet Brar) is an undervalued clutch weapon that throttles run rates in high-leverage middle overs.
4. **Rajasthan Royals Strategic Alignment**:
   - Recommends pairing late-overs boundary hitters with positive PRC slopes (Romario Shepherd, Jason Holder) with extreme-pressure death bowlers who suppress boundary rates below 12% (Nathan Ellis, Matheesha Pathirana).
