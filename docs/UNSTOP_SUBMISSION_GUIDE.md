## 3. Executive Summary Text for Submission Portal

*(Word count: ~250 words — Ideal for copying into the "Methodology / Approach" field on Unstop)*

> **PRESSUREIQ** is a context-aware cricket performance and reliability intelligence framework developed for the SupeRR Selector Season 2 competition using ball-by-ball delivery records from the IPL and Men's T20Is (2022–2026, 200,999 deliveries across 867 matches).
>
> Rather than relying on unadjusted aggregates (raw strike rates or career economy, which suffer from low-stakes stat-padding), PRESSUREIQ defines pressure as contextual match difficulty on a continuous 0–100 scale: **Batting Pressure Index (BPI)** and **Bowling Pressure Index (BoPI)**. Contextual expectations ($E[\text{runs}]$, $P(\text{dismissal})$, $P(\text{boundary})$) are generated using strictly chronological walk-forward out-of-fold LightGBM models with probability calibration ($Brier \approx 0.05, ECE < 0.009$) and zero data leakage. Shortened matches incorporate dynamic legal-ball allocations for both innings.
>
> Delivery-level **Pressure-Adjusted Value Added (PAVA)** incorporates complete accounting of illegal deliveries (charging wides/no-balls to bowlers) and decomposes player reliability into **Five Quantitative Pillars**: Absolute Pressure PAVA, Baseline Retention Delta, Opportunity-Adjusted Consistency, Context Adaptability (IPL vs T20I), and Empirical Bayes Shrinkage ($K=45$ pseudo-balls calibrated from variance ratios). Furthermore, **Pressure Response Curves (PRC)** classify players into empirical archetypes with statistical significance gating.
>
> Ranking stability is verified through **2,000 match-clustered bootstrap resamples** with exact scoring mathematical equivalence ($k=\mathbf{1}$ reproduces baseline scores to $<0.05$). The complete framework includes an interactive Streamlit/Plotly dashboard, exhaustive audit reports, and a 40-test verification suite passing with 100% success.

## 4. Key Talking Points for Presentation / Interview Round

1. **Why Raw Strike Rate and Economy are Misleading**:
   - In our ablation study, raw career strike rate has only a **10% overlap** with pressure reliability rankings. Traditional metrics reward Powerplay harvesting against field restrictions and low-pressure dead rubbers, whereas pressure reliability measures rate value added when wickets have fallen or required run rates exceed 10.0 RPO.
2. **Empirical Bayes Shrinkage ($K=45$)**:
   - Derived mathematically from the variance ratio of within-player delivery noise ($\sigma^2_{\epsilon} \approx 1.8$) to between-player true skill variance ($\sigma^2_{\theta} \approx 0.04$). Small samples are regressed to the league prior, preventing 15-ball anomalies from distorting rankings.
3. **Pace vs Spin Under Pressure**:
   - While express pace yorkers (Matheesha Pathirana, Haris Rauf, Nathan Ellis) dominate high-pressure death overs, defensive spin (Liam Dawson, Sufiyan Muqeem, Nasum Ahmed) provides crucial high-leverage choke points that throttle middle-overs scoring.
4. **Illegal Delivery Accounting**:
   - Bowlers are penalized for conceded runs on wides and no-balls while legal deliveries serve as the exposure denominator, ensuring discipline under pressure is quantitatively rewarded.
